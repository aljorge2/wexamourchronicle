On the night of the vigil, I wrote that a voice from the station doors called for the flames to be put out, and that I could not see who it was in the doorway.

This week someone who works in the Office's record rooms showed me the Vaelthir station's order book, where each night's orders are written and signed by whoever gives them. This is the entry for Day 219:

> Day 219. Station steps. Clear them. Use what force is required.
> E. Thatch, Magistrate, at the Chair's instruction.

I set it beside the writ the Magistrate signed for the Bureau's raid on the Faded Lands Board. It is the same hand.

There are two E. Thatches in this story. One was standing on the steps with a list of the dead, asking for five minutes. The other wrote "Magistrate" after his name and sent the line down on her.

## The four words at the end

He did not give the order on his own. The serpent-mousers that killed twenty on the strip and four on the square were bought from the Civil Compliance Initiative, which answers to the Chair alone. The messages printed here last week show the Chair asking for the dead to be buried "and be done with it," and signing it S.

The Office has stripped Emory Thatch of his title over his wife's empty cell. It has said nothing about the order he wrote.
