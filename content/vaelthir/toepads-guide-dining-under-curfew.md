Vaelthir has always eaten late, and so the curfew has asked more of our kitchens than of anyone, and I am pleased to report that they have risen to it. The four o'clock sitting is the new eight o'clock, and the good tables go fast, so book it the day before. Freeman's is calling it curfew supper and serving the full evening menu from three, and Fenmore's Taco Club will pack any order into a paper box to carry home, which is the most civilized thing to happen to a basilisk carnita in years. Order the soup first, since it will have cooled to a sensible temperature by the time you have finished watching the clock. Pay before the pudding, not after, so you are not caught with a spoon in your hand when the light starts to go. Most rooms now draw their shutters at dusk rather than show a light to the street, so if you like a window seat, take it early. And tip as though your server has somewhere to be, because they do, and most of them have farther to walk home than you.

My only complaint this week is the bread at Freeman's, which was the best on the square a fortnight ago and is now merely bread. I am told it is a new hand at the ovens. The old one has not come in since the searches began on the strip, and that is all I will say on that.
