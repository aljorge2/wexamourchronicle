I should say at the top that I did not like Godfrey Ashmere and I am not going to pretend I did. However, I am going to report his death the way I would report anyone's.

His housekeeper found him dead in his study on Cressmere Lane late last night. By noon the Bureau had taken the body and sealed the house. The Office has not said how he died; it has only said who it blames: "persons associated with the Archaeological Society."

Two things have been found since, and they do not agree.

## The door

Someone cut a Claddagh into his front door overnight: two hands, a heart, a crown. On his desk his housekeeper found last week's page, folded once. GODFREY ASHMERE IS A TYRANT.

## The inbox

At dusk, a few hours before he was found, a message reached his inbox from Chair Stilver Lugrathe, the man he had been writing to all season:

> I would like a word tonight. S.

Within hours of his death, every message in that inbox was deleted. The deletions came from s.lugrathe@orcm.gov.wx.

I do not know who killed Godfrey Ashmere. He told me once that he would rather be wrong beside the Office than right beside a crowd. I do not know which of them was beside him at the end.

*Merritt Vance reports for the Wexamour Archaeological Society. If you saw anyone on Cressmere Lane on the night of Day 235, write to him by owl.*
