The Whispering Groves Council has declared a state of emergency across the district, effective at once, as a wild overgrowth of vines and trees swallows the region. All trail systems, including the DellRiven and Ashridge networks, are closed until further notice, and residents are asked to keep to cleared ground, to leave the deep wood alone, and to send word to the council by owl if the growth reaches their homes so that hands can be sent to help clear it. The council said it could not say what was driving the growth, only that it was spreading in a way no ordinary season could account for. Until we know more the council asks that neighbours help one another, mind the children and the old folk, and look in on anyone living alone at the forest's edge.
