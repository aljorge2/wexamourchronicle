The Groves are busier than they have been in years, and with the creeks running high and the tuskboars pushed up to the townline, the old growth asks a little more care of you this season than it usually does. None of what follows should keep you off the finest country in Wexamour. It is only meant to see you home at the end of the day. A few simple habits will do most of the work.

**Never walk alone.** Go in company, always. The trail is friendliest in a group, and on a quiet stretch you never quite know who might slither up alongside you.

**Bring a good hunting beast if you can.** A well-bred manticore is worth its weight out here, for it is bred for snakes above all things and will scent one long before you ever could. If you do not keep one, walk with someone who does.

**Keep to open, cairned ground.** Stay out of the tall grass and the loose rockfalls, the shaded, coiled-up places where a thing can lie out of the sun and wait for the warmth of you to pass.

**Watch the still water and the warm flat rocks.** The cold-blooded like to bask where it is quiet. If something is lying too still and watching you go by, do not linger to learn its intentions. Keep moving.

**Wear good high boots.** Thick leather to the knee, for the obvious reasons.

**Walk heavy and let the country hear you.** Most things that would do you harm would far rather not be found, and will clear off the path if you give them fair warning you are coming.

**Do not trust a smooth talker.** If a stranger falls in beside you all charm and forked words, offering to show you a shortcut the maps do not know, keep your own counsel and your own road.

The Groves are for everyone who respects them. Enjoy them this season, watch your footing, watch your company, and mind what shares the trail with you.
