The Groves are busier than they have been in years, and with that the old growth asks a little more care of you this season than it usually does. None of what follows should keep you off the finest country in Wexamour. Below is a list of a few simple things that will help see you home at the end of the day.

<strong style="color:#b8860b;">Never walk alone.</strong> You never quite know who might slither up alongside you.

<strong style="color:#b8860b;">Bring a good hunting companion if you can.</strong> A well-bred manticore is worth its weight out here, for it is bred for snakes above all things and will scent one long before you ever could.

<strong style="color:#b8860b;">Do not trust easily.</strong> If a stranger falls in beside you offering directions and tips unprompted, do not listen. Stick to what you know instead of a hissing smooth talker.

The Groves are for everyone who respects them. Enjoy them this season, watch your footing, watch your company, and mind what shares the trail with you.
