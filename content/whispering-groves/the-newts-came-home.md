The vines stopped growing the night the clocks started. Households along Ashwinder Creek that were losing a room a night finally caught a break, and by morning the growth on every door in the Groves had ceased and had gone soft enough to pull by hand.

So the Groves are cutting their way out. The Council has crews on the Vaelthir road and the main stair of Grove Hospital, paid for at last from the Office's new emergency funds, and every household with a blade is clearing its own door and then its neighbour's. A good share of the cuttings has gone south by cart to be woven into windbreaks around the phoenix ash. The DellRiven and Ashridge networks will reopen trail by trail, and the hospital once its wards are clear.

On the first night the Vaelthir road was cleared at the Ashridge turning, the redbelly newts crossed it on their way to Lake Viralora, thousands of them, for the first time in years. Families stood along the verge with lamps so no cart would roll through.

*Kael Dunwick is the Whispering Groves section's backcountry travel and gear correspondent. Households that need hands to clear their doors, or have hands to spare, can write by owl.*
