I have walked every marked trail in the Groves at least twice, and this week I could not walk a single one of them. The paths I know by heart have grown shut in a matter of nights, and since the trees came up through the wards of Grove Hospital I have spent every morning doing what most of you have been doing, which is hacking a way out of my own front door. Nobody has yet told us what is driving this or when it will stop, but we do not need to know either to look after our own doorsteps.

1. **Clear first thing in the morning.** Get to it before the night's growth has had time to set hard, because a vine you can snap at dawn wants a saw by supper. Keep a path open to your door, your well, and your nearest neighbour, in that order, and do not try to win back the whole garden.
2. **Keep your tools sharp, and share them.** Sharpen your loppers and billhooks, and if you have more than one, lend the spare to someone who has none.
3. **Pile your cuttings well away from the house.** Take them out to the end of your lane. Left up against your walls, they will root again overnight.
4. **Look in on your neighbours.** Check each morning on anyone living alone at the forest's edge, and if the growth has reached their windows, go and help them clear it.

<br>

And please, however much the old paths call to you, stay out of the deep wood. Whatever is driving this is coming from in there, and none of us yet knows what it is.
