On the first of the month the Whispering Groves Council wrote to the Office of Resource and Civil Management asking for officers, wagons, and cutting tools to help clear the overgrowth that has held the district under a state of emergency since the twenty-fourth of August. The office has answered. It regrets that it cannot assist at this time, as the Bureau's available officers have been reassigned to "matters of public order in Vaelthir," and it advises the council to make use of local resources. The council would note, for the record, that local resources are what we have been using. Grove Hospital remains closed. The growth has now crossed the Vaelthir road at the Ashridge turning, and households along Ashwinder Creek report losing a room a night. Until help comes from somewhere, the council asks every household with a blade to lend or a pair of hands to spare to send word by owl, and asks that neighbours keep clearing for one another as they have been, because the vines do not keep a curfew.

*Households that need hands to clear their doors, or that have tools or hands to spare, can write to the Whispering Groves Council by owl.*
