Twenty of our serpent-folk neighbours were killed on the strip last week. The deaths began the first night the Bureau's serpent-mousers started their patrols. Some of those we lost were walking home or still at work, some were in their own yards. The list of our friends is below.

- **Prewitt Hale, 58.** Prewitt had just come home from Grove Hospital and was still mending. He was sitting on his own porch step when the mouser came over the fence.
- **Tolley Harrow, 31.** Tolley was walking from the Byre place with a pot of mouse soup for his new daughter, he was a quarter mile from his door. Wenna (his wife) and the baby are alive.
- **Pip Coombes, 7.** Pip grew up with a manticore in the house, the Coombes' own pet that a patrol officer fetched out of an elm only weeks ago. When a mouser came down the lane, Pip went to pet it and was attacked. Her mother was ten steps behind her.
- **Denner Byre, 44, and his son Ossie Byre, 11.** Denner and Ossie were out at dusk watering the one green strip on the far corner of their land. They were found together clearly attacked from behind.
- **Old Ferris Down, 81.** Ferris was drawing water at his own recently mended well.
- **Hester Attlebury, 52.** Hester was cutting what was left of the Attlebury plot, alone, because the neighbours who helped her last time were keeping indoors as we had asked.
- **Wynn Castellan, 16, and Tobin Critch, 15.** Wynn and Tobin were walking home together from Silverwolf High, they were both a part of the Song Society and helped plan Deepsong.
- **Airedale Thornby, 17.** Since the spring prom, she has not been able to run even a quarter mile without stopping to rest. She could not get away when the mousers chased her. Her mother has now buried a husband and a daughter in one season.
- **Emmit Rourke, 39.** Emmit was coming back up the second-creek lane after supper with his brother. His brother lived but is in critical condition. Emmit did not.
- **Maudie Kettleby, 68, and Nils Ambry, 47.** Maudie and Nils were walking home from work together. Maudie was the strip's midwife and had spent another long shift monitoring Wenna Harrow and the baby. Nils had spent the day clearing spoiled stores for T. Ashe.
- **Sable Orrin, 34.** Sable lit the lamps on the south road every evening so the rest of us could see our way home. She only got to the fourth lamp.
- **Harlan Denby, 57.** Harlan was pulling the last of his dead crops on the low farm past the old mile-marker.
- **Ivy, Sela's girl, 22.** Ivy was walking home from the well at dusk, the way she has every evening of her life. Nettie Combe was the one who found her.
- **Alma Thresher, 63.** Alma was at her cellar door at dusk, taking stock of what little Rain had left her. She never did get her copper pot back.
- **Isolde Marsh, 38.** Isolde stepped out onto her porch to call her children in for the night. Her children found her dead on the porch.
- **Hollis Barrow, 46.** Hollis was out past the second creek at dusk, she was taking notes for a story about the animals and creatures sitting on our southern border.
- **Wendel Pike, 76.** Wendel was walking the fence line along the low road after supper. He is survived by his daughter Nettie Combe.

We are begging for an independent investigation into every one of these deaths, run by someone outside of both the Wexamour Bureau and the Office of Resource and Civil Management. We have sent numerous messages both by phone and owl and heard nothing. We are only a community board we have no independent officers and no authority. To the laypeople who read this, please use your voice to influence those in power to stop the killings. We cannot bury twenty more.

*If you witnessed any of last week's deaths, lost someone, or have seen where the patrols are being run from, send word to the Faded Lands Community Board by owl. Your account will be held in confidence. If you can offer the families food, a bed, or a hand with a burial, write to us and we will pass it on.*
