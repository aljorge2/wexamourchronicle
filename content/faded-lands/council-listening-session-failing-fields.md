A couple articles recently came out here about wilting crops and spoiling stores. In response, the town council has agreed to do the sit down and listen to concerns to establish a pattern. There will be a listening session at the meeting hall this coming week, at the second hour after noon, open to any farmer or grower or concerned citizen whose crop has died this season. Come and say what you have lost, and what you have tried. A clerk will be there to take it all down.

We know a listening session is not a healer, but we want these accounts to be recorded so we can give it to the regional offices, so it cannot be waved off as one unlucky farm. We are here for you and please help us help you by telling us everything you know.
