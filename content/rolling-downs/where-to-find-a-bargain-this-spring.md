Every spring somebody asks me where the deals are, and every spring I tell them the same thing, which is that a deal is only a deal if you needed the thing already. That said, the Millhaven market has been livelier than it has any right to be lately, so here is where I would point your basket this week.

Honey, I am sorry to say, is still dear, and will stay dear while the whole region goes on brawling over Dorran's ridge. Eggs likewise. But root vegetables have come in cheap and plentiful this season, cheaper than I have seen in years, and if anyone knows why the ground has been so generous with turnips of all things, they are keeping it to themselves. Buy them while they last, and do not ask too many questions of a bargain.

For everything else: Tamsin at the wool stall is clearing last season's dyed skeins at half, the tinker's-workshop folk are mending kettles two for the price of one, and Freeman's still has a table of beast-day oddments going for a song, though you will have to forgive the faint smell of manticore. Bring your own sack, bring your patience, and mind you are home before the market lamps go down.
