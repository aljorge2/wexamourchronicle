A small word for the neighbours. The mending circle is meeting again this rest-day at the Millhaven Grange Hall, the hour after supper, the way it always has. Bring whatever has gone torn or thin over this long season, and bring yourself besides, for the mending was never really the whole of the point of it.

There is a seat for anyone who has been feeling the quiet lately, and tea enough for twice who we expect. Come as you are, and be counted among friends. We keep a lamp in the window for anyone who runs late on the road.
