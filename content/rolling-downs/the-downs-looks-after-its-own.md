I see they are passing the hat again. There is a fundraiser for the serpent-folk farmers down in the Faded Lands who lost their crops, and Luna and Freeman's Windward Bestiary are behind it, hauling the cute animals out onto the green to draw a crowd and asking every good soul on the Downs to open a purse as if we have the money to spare.

I am sorry for a hard season. I am. But I did not sour anyone's milk and I did not wilt anyone's field. What I know is that the ground down there has been worked by the serpent-folk longer than any of us have been alive, and it is their ground that turned, on their watch, this spring of all springs, and now the bill for it is being carried up our roads with a smile. You may call me hard. I call it noticing. When a thing goes wrong everywhere the same hands have been, a body starts to wonder about the hands.

Luna means well. Luna always means well. She handed the key to the city back rather than keep a thing she felt she had not earned, and I respect her for it, and she would fundraise for the tuskboars too if you let her stand still long enough. Now she has got Freeman's trotting its manticores round the green to raise coin for serpent-folk, which is a joke that very near tells itself, and the softer half of the Downs is lining up to coo at the beasts and drop their coppers in the tin. Bless them. They will feel very good about it tonight.

Here is what I keep thinking. A season ago this paper would have printed that fundraiser notice up at the top of the page and asked you kindly to give, and I would have had to write my piece as a letter they might not have run. It is a new day at this paper, and I am glad of it, glad there is finally room to say plainly that charity begins at your own gate. Keep your coin for your own people. The strip can look after the strip.

*Merrigan Ploughwell farms the middle fields outside Brannock Crossing and now writes a regular column for the Rolling Downs pages. She can be reached by owl, or by standing in the lane and hollering, which she does anyway.*
