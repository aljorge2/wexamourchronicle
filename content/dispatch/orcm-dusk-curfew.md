The Office of Resource and Civil Management has declared a curfew from dusk to dawn across the Vaelthir University square, the streets surrounding its Vaelthir station, and all public roads on the Faded Lands strip, effective immediately and until further notice. Under the order, no person may be abroad in the affected areas between dusk and dawn without leave from the office, with the exception of Bureau officers on patrol and the animals in their keeping. Gatherings of more than five persons in any public place now require a permit, to be applied for in person at the Vaelthir station at a fee of $40. Lanterns, torches, and other open flame are barred in public after dusk. Bureau patrols on the strip will be doubled, and officers are authorized to enter and search any premises where there is reason to believe a person sought by the office is being sheltered. The south road is closed to all traffic beyond the second creek, at any hour, and officers have been posted at the crossing to turn travellers back. The office did not give a reason for the closure. Announcing the measures, ORCM Chair Stilver Lugrathe said the order would remain in force for as long as the region required it. "My daughter and the man who took her are believed to be sheltered by sympathizers on the strip," he said. "Whoever is hiding them is not protecting her. They are keeping a sick girl from her father, and they will answer for it."
