The Office of Resource and Civil Management has confirmed that it has placed a standing order for serpent-mousers, the manticore breed that has been in such demand across the region this season, to be stationed at its waystations and issued to officers working the roads of the Faded Lands and the Whispering Groves. The office described the commission as "a routine measure for the safety of our personnel and posts."

Asked what had made the roads unsafe enough to require them, a spokesperson said only that the office "does not characterise operational matters," that the measure was "precautionary," and that residents would be advised of any change to conditions "as and when appropriate." The spokesperson would not say whether the commission was connected to the concerns farmers and walkers have raised about who has been seen on the roads after dark.

The order was placed with the Entmoot conservatory and several other Groves breeders, whose waiting lists were already long with private buyers, and has by several accounts made those lists longer still. What had until this week been passed around as rumour, and as the ordinary wariness of a hard season, is now a matter of standing policy.
