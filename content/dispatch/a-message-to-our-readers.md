We write to inform our readers of a change in the stewardship of this paper. Effective this week, Godfrey Ashmere will assume the position of Editor of the Wexamour Chronicle, with the full confidence of the paper's proprietors and a mandate to bring our coverage into closer keeping with the standards of accuracy, responsibility, and institutional consistency that these times require. We thank the outgoing editorial leadership for their years of service. There may be some changes in what we cover and in how we cover it, and we ask for your patience and your trust as those changes take effect. The Chronicle remains, as it has always been, committed to serving the public good and keeping everyone informed.
