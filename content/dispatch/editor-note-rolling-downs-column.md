A column appeared recently in our Rolling Downs pages, under the name Luna, concerning the difficulties on the Faded Lands strip and the response of this community to them. It advanced a number of claims that this paper is not in a position to substantiate and does not endorse, and it has accordingly been withdrawn from our pages. We regret any distress its publication may have caused our readers.

Let me be clear that we hold Luna in the highest regard. Her service to the Downs through the flooding is a matter of record, and no one who watched her run that relief yard would question her good heart. It is precisely because of that regard that we say, plainly, that a good heart is not the same as good information, and that the matters she took up are not ones on which she is equipped to speak. Warmth is a fine thing in a neighbour. It is not a substitute for verification in these pages.

The Chronicle's position is unchanged. We stand with the ordinary safety of the people we serve, and with the concerns they have raised, again and again, about their roads and their fields. We will continue to report those concerns faithfully. We ask, as ever, for the patience and the trust of our readers as we hold this paper to the standard these times require.
