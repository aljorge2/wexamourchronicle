Stilver Lugrathe, Acting Chair of the Office of Resource and Civil Management, was arrested this morning at his home at 7 Thornveil Court and charged by the Vaelthir Regional Magistracy with obtaining money under false pretences. In the spring he opened a fundraiser on Almshearth asking the region to "help Ikavelth recover and cover her hospital care." More than a hundred and eighty people gave and the page raised more than twenty-four thousand dollars.

Grove Hospital's itemized statement for his daughter, obtained by this paper, shows payments received: $0.00. On Day 112 he told the hospital not to file a claim with his own office's insurer, and on Day 113 he declined financial assistance on her behalf. On Day 131 he telephoned for her balance "for a fundraising page." On Day 152 he told the hospital the funds "have been raised." On Day 171 he refused a payment plan, saying the money was "reserved for my daughter's recovery," and the same day he had the debt moved onto her as the adult of record. The balance of $67,423.57 was referred to the Civil Debt Division in her name on Day 224. More than twenty thousand dollars of it was for charges he had ordered himself: an ORCM guard on her door, a visitor list with only his name on it, and a bed held open on Ward ED-3 at $95 a night, waiting for her to be brought back.

He was taken from the house by Constable Brina Lowe, badge 0347, who stood in that same sitting room on the night his daughter was admitted. The Magistracy has voided the debt and released the bed. Almshearth says it has found the fundraiser to be false and, under its Giving Guarantee, is returning every pledge in full. Readers who gave will see their money back this week, and the hospital, which never received a coin, has been told not to expect one from him. On the step he said only one thing to Constable Lowe: "Everything I did, I did for her."
