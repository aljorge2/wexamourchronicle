Edna Thatch, president of the Wexamour Archaeological Society, has been named Chair of the Office of Resource and Civil Management following the arrest of Stilver Lugrathe. She is the first person to hold the title without the word "Acting" in front of it in living memory.

Mrs. Thatch was named by the Vaelthir Regional Magistracy at the written request of the office's own staff. The letter was signed by more than two hundred clerks, wardens and keepers of the record.

She signed seven orders on her first morning. The curfew on the square and the strip is lifted. The serpent-mousers are recalled to the Entmoot conservatory, and Operation MantiMouser is ended. The Plateau Research Station is closed and its wardens stood down. Every record reclassified under Session 997, including the early-period documents on the Silence and every geographical survey held in the University archive, is returned to public access. The records the office sealed under Internal Directive 18-C are unsealed. The Bureau is ordered to say where its wagons took the thirty dead from under the University wall on Day 220, and to return every one of them to their families. She also opened up emergency funds for the Faded Lands and Whispering Groves to rebuild their districts.

Asked what she intends to do with the office, Mrs. Thatch said: "I have spent my whole life finding what other people misplaced. I intend to give it back."
