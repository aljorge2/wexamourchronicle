A column appeared recently in our Vaelthir pages under the name of Edna Thatch, and I want to be plain about how it got there. Mrs. Thatch was invited to introduce herself as the new president of the Wexamour Archaeological Society. She was asked, politely and in writing, to keep it apolitical. She agreed. She then used the space this paper gave her to call readers into the street outside the Office of Resource and Civil Management, to urge that office's own clerks and wardens to abandon their posts, and to lecture this paper about a column we had already withdrawn. That is not an introduction. That is an ambush, and it will not happen in these pages again. The Chronicle does not print calls for public servants to walk off the job in a season when this region can least afford it, and the column has been withdrawn. We regret the distress it caused our readers, and I regret that it ran at all. Let me be clear that I have held Mrs. Thatch in high regard for many years, and her service behind the library desk is a matter of record. But good service at a desk does not give anyone the right to turn this masthead into a picket sign. Effective immediately, Mrs. Thatch is blacklisted from writing for the Chronicle in any capacity, and any further submissions under her name will be returned unread. We thank her for her contribution to these pages.
