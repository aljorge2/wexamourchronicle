Something a little different from the Hour today, and from the Chronicle as a whole. We are going to start making games for you!

It is called The Beastiary Clades, and you can [play it here](/clade-game/). You get two beasts, real or fabled, and you have to name the clade where their bloodlines meet on the tree of life. There is a Daily Clade if you just want one puzzle with your morning tea, and a few longer modes if you want to lose an afternoon to it.

Here is where you come in. This is a beta, so nothing about it is precious yet. Play it, then come back to this thread and tell us everything. What was fun, what was confusing, what broke, what was too easy or too hard. If something does not work, say so plainly. If you loved it, we would like to hear that too, so we know what to make more of.

Drop your notes in the comments below! The best of them go straight to the people building the next one.
